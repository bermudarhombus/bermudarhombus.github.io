About

Tony Regli is a first-year graduate student in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering studying how dynamical systems theory can be applied to orbital dynamics in multi-body environments. He received his Bachelors degree in Mathematics and Aerospace Engineering from the University of Maryland: College Park, where he worked with the UMD Collective Dynamics and Controls Laboratory on development of computer vision software for multi-robot systems. During his summers in undergrad, Tony helped develop optimal state estimation toolkits for inertial navigation systems as a Guidance, Controls, and Navigation intern at the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Lab. Outside of class, he spends most of his time reading, cooking, or playing classical guitar.
